Before you book

Straight answers.

Launch-range sites usually ship in 3–4 weeks. Growth-range (shop, booking, longer path) is about 5 weeks. The clock starts after the call, when the offer and pages are clear — not after a vague “we should do a website.”

Copy structure, design, mobile layout, the one conversion path, and launch. You get a site that can take money or bookings — not a moodboard. Extra systems (CMS, shop, membership) are what you check in the calculator.

Care is hosting, updates, and keeping the path working after launch. The calculator shows a monthly number next to the build. You can skip it and host it yourself; most people don’t, because a dead plugin costs more than care.
