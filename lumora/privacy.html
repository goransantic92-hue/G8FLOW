Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

Short version: I collect the details you choose to send me so I can reply about a project. I don't sell them, I don't run advertising trackers, and I delete them when they're no longer needed.

Who is responsible

This site is operated by G8 Flow (Goran Šantić), a freelance web design and development practice based in Serbia. For any privacy question, write to goransantic92@gmail.com.

What I collect and why

Inquiry form

When you submit the form in the pricing section, I receive the name, email address, budget note, project description, and the estimate the calculator produced. I use it only to answer your inquiry and to prepare a quote. The submission is delivered to my inbox by Resend, an email delivery provider acting on my behalf.

Booking a call

The "Book a call" buttons open Calendly. When you schedule, Calendly processes the details you enter under its own privacy notice and shares the booking with me.

Analytics

I use Vercel Web Analytics to see which pages and buttons get used. It is cookie-free and does not build a profile of you or follow you across other sites. It records aggregate signals such as page path, referrer, country, device type, and which calls-to-action were clicked. See the Vercel Analytics privacy notice.

Server logs

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps short-lived technical logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps) for security and abuse prevention. The inquiry endpoint also uses your IP address in memory for a few seconds to throttle spam submissions; it is not stored.

What I don't do

  • No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies
  • No selling, renting, or trading your data
  • No newsletter sign-up hidden inside the inquiry form
  • No automated decision-making or profiling

Legal basis

Where the GDPR applies, I rely on your consent and on my legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries and keeping the site secure and functional (Art. 6(1)(a) and 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How long I keep it

Inquiry emails are kept for as long as the conversation is commercially relevant, and up to 24 months after my last contact, then deleted. Records tied to a signed project are kept for as long as accounting rules require. Analytics data is aggregate and retained by Vercel per its own retention schedule.

Who else sees it

Only the providers needed to run the site and reply to you: Vercel (hosting, analytics), Resend (email delivery), Calendly (scheduling), and my email provider. I don't share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask me to show you the data I hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict how I use it, or send it to you in a portable format. You can also withdraw consent at any time and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Email goransantic92@gmail.com and I'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
